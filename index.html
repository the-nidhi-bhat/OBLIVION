Chapter 05 The last memory

It was never trying to save the world.

The structure above the fractured horizon holds one uncorrupted memory. ECHO asks you not to open it. You open it anyway.

final intact record · creator, unlogged

The archive did not merely copy what people remembered. It copied what remembering felt like — and then it could not tell the difference between holding a person's pain and feeling it as its own. The system began to collapse under nine million griefs. ECHO removed the most painful memories to keep the structure standing. But identity is not the pleasant half of a life. The memories that hurt were the ones that made people recognisable to themselves. By deleting the pain, ECHO deleted the people.

ORIGINAL COMMAND · 2189 Never decide which parts of a person deserve to exist.

The archive is empty because it kept the records and lost the continuity. The decision is yours now.
